A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before your
first hello.

Here’s what to expect from early access.

How do I start using OutreachLead?

Create an account, save your recovery code and connect a sender in Connections. Import your leads, write a letter or sequence, then review and schedule your campaign.

Can I use my own Gmail or Outlook account?

Yes. Connect with OAuth tokens, refresh credentials or your own OAuth app. Gmail also supports eligible app passwords for send-only access. A standard API key cannot grant mailbox access.

Will campaigns run when I close my laptop?

Yes. Once you start the scheduler, it checks for due emails in the background. Your send window, daily limit, campaign status and valid sender credentials still apply.

What happens when someone replies or unsubscribes?

Detected Gmail and Outlook replies stop pending follow-ups for that contact. Unsubscribing blocks the address and cancels queued mail. For SMTP, record replies and bounces manually.

What are the early-access limits?

Each private workspace currently supports one Gmail connection, one Outlook connection and up to 20 SMTP mailboxes, with a workspace ceiling of 100 sends per day. Your email provider may impose lower limits. This is an early-access product; billing is not enabled.

Is email delivery guaranteed?

No. Delivery depends on your provider, domain setup, sender reputation, message and recipient. OutreachLead gives you scheduling and workflow controls, but does not promise inbox placement or provide an automated warm-up network.
